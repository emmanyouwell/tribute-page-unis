UNIS' Journey

Pre-debut to First Award

  • 2023
    • January - SBS announced their first survival show, Universe Ticket.
    • March 2 - SBS opened the audition form to the public for any girl who has been born before the year 2011 regardless their nationality. The audtion lasted from March 2 to May 14, 2023.
    • July 13 & 17 - Contestants were revealed through profile teaser images.
    • November - The show, Universe Ticket, officially aired.
  • 2024
    • January 10 - Elisia was confirmed as the first member following her advancement to P rank in the ninth episode.
    • March 27 - UNIS debuted with their debut mini album, "WE UNIS".
    • August 6 - UNIS made their first-ever comeback with their first single album "Curious".
    • August 22 - UNIS won 2 awards in the 2024 KWDA.
      1. 🏆 Music iKON Award
      2. 🏆 Girl Group Popularity Award
